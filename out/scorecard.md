# What translates and what doesn't: Combine → regular-season scorecard

Generated by `scorecard.py` from the saved robust outputs (METRICS.md §9). ρ is oriented so **+ = a better athlete goes with a better result**. Year-controlled ρ is the raw link: both sides demeaned within draft year, on the same outcome as the size-controlled fit (shrunk rate where one exists), nothing else held fixed (METRICS.md §9, audit fix 11/2026; the as-reported-rate version is in `scorecard_full.csv` as `rho_asreported_yearctrl`). Size-controlled ρ also holds weight and height fixed (DB also CB vs S; DL also Edge vs Interior). q = BH false-discovery rate over the full family; ΔR² = held-out-draft-class gain over a size-only model (classes that improved). Draft slot is never used.

## Selection and caveats

- One row per group × result: the test with the smallest permutation p among tests with n ≥ 25 (rule fixed in METRICS.md §9). The full grid is `out/scorecard_full.csv`.
- SIZE, NOT SPEED rows in the full grid: 25 (all DL; 23 on the card). Get-off speed rows are the clearest case: year-controlled ρ of about +0.65 to +0.78 falls to roughly +0.06 to +0.20 once weight, height and role are held fixed.
- Multiple testing: the grid has 164 cells with n ≥ 25; about 8 would reach perm p < 0.05 by chance alone, and 28 do. Only 2 clear q < 0.05 (the TRANSLATES rows). LEANS rows are nominal-p findings and should be read as leads. The card shows the best-p test per result, so card ρ values are optimistic by construction.
- Confidence intervals are 2,000-resample percentile bootstraps over players (the whole fit is redone in each resample). Below n = 15 the bootstrap is unreliable and the dashboard hides the interval.
- WR break-route separation comes from the pre-declared re-test (METRICS.md §8); the broader break family in the first robust pass is not used.
- Same 2023–25 players generated the leads and test them; held-out draft classes are the only out-of-sample check, and there are three.
- A TOO FEW or DOESN'T TRANSLATE verdict is absence of evidence at this sample size, not proof of no effect.
